Utterance level, not streaming. The translation starts when the speaker stops talking. "Ready after" is measured on this machine, from the end of the utterance to the translated audio being ready.
AI fallback is on (if the badge says RECORDED, the AI answers are played back from an earlier real run, not asked live). When the rules cannot translate a whole sentence, an AI model (Gnani Evon, self-hosted) is asked. Its answer is spoken only if a fixed check finds that left/right, negation, question, condition, colours, numbers, and who is standing, coming or “where” match what was said; those turns are labelled AI translated. The check does not cover the rest of the meaning, so the AI can still be wrong, and the Kannada has not been reviewed by a native speaker.
